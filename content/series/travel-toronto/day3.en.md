## Casa Loma

Maybe it's because I especially love old buildings, but Casa Loma was my number one favorite place from this whole Toronto trip.

Casa Loma is a privately built castle. The original owner later went bankrupt, and after a lot of ups and downs, the castle is now owned by the City of Toronto and open to the public.  
Inside, you can see living quarters, a grand hall, a conservatory, a library, stables, and towers. At the entrance, there are maps and guide materials available in different languages.

Casa Loma has two towers. Unfortunately, when I visited, the Norman Tower was closed due to the weather.  
The iron spiral staircase in the tower was kind of scary to climb, but the view from the top overlooks the Toronto skyline, so if you're not afraid of heights, I definitely recommend going up.

After exploring the first and second floors and the tower, there was also a gift shop in the basement plus the Dark Side Tunnel leading toward the stables. It felt like walking through an underground secret passage, which was really fun.

## Old City Hall and City Hall

These two spots are right next to each other. You can pretty much see both just by crossing the street.

Old City Hall is even prettier on the outside than Casa Loma, but when I went, the road in front was under construction, so it was hard to find a good angle for photos. I also couldn't find clear info online about whether it was open to visitors, so I could only walk past and admire it from the outside.

## Eaton Building and Sankofa Square

Basically, this area is for shopping. You can find pretty much every brand people might want to buy while visiting Canada: Roots, Aritzia, Arc'teryx, Lululemon, and even MUJI.

## PAI

PAI is a well-known Thai restaurant in Toronto. This time I ordered the Thai green curry.  
The curry itself came in a coconut, and the coconut meat inside was really good. The rice was wrapped in a leaf, so the whole presentation looked very impressive.  
Compared with the green curry I was used to, this one was a bit thinner, but the flavor was great, the rice was good, and the meat portion was very generous.
