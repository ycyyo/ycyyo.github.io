Overall, I really liked Toronto—maybe because you hear Chinese everywhere. In Montréal, I’d get excited just hearing someone speak Chinese. In Toronto, it turned into hoping I’d hear a Taiwanese accent instead.

Day 1: Koreatown -> Kensington Market -> Chinatown -> The Planet Traveler Hostel
Day 2: Niagara Falls -> Distillery District  
Day 3: Casa Loma -> Old City Hall -> City Hall -> Eaton Centre -> Sankofa Square -> PAI  
Day 4: University of Toronto -> Royal Ontario Museum -> CN Tower  
Day 5: St. Lawrence Market

If you’re not planning to visit places like the aquarium, I think three full days is actually enough to cover Niagara Falls and most of the city. (Day 1 and Day 5 both included about half a day of train travel.)
